Maritime Defense Solutions

Advanced Maritime Security Technologies

DBF Science Technology specializes in comprehensive maritime defense solutions, combining advanced radar systems, underwater sensors, and AI-driven analytics to provide superior maritime domain awareness and protection capabilities.

Maritime Defense Capabilities

Surface Surveillance

  • SAR Ship Detection: Advanced synthetic aperture radar for all-weather vessel detection
  • Coastal Radar Systems: High-frequency surface wave radar for extended range monitoring
  • AIS Integration: Automatic identification system correlation and anomaly detection
  • Small Craft Detection: Specialized systems for detecting low-profile and stealth vessels

Underwater Defense

  • Sonar Networks: Multi-static sonar arrays for submarine and diver detection
  • Acoustic Monitoring: Passive acoustic systems for underwater threat identification
  • Underwater Barriers: Physical and electronic underwater perimeter protection
  • Mine Detection: Advanced sonar and magnetic systems for naval mine identification

Port & Harbor Security

  • Integrated Security Systems: Comprehensive port protection and access control
  • Vessel Traffic Management: Advanced VTS systems with AI-enhanced decision support
  • Cargo Screening: Non-intrusive inspection systems for containers and cargo
  • Perimeter Protection: Multi-layered security zones with automated threat response

Maritime Intelligence

  • Pattern-of-Life Analysis: Behavioral modeling for vessel tracking and prediction
  • Dark Target Detection: Identification of vessels operating without transponders
  • Maritime Domain Awareness: Comprehensive situational picture generation
  • Threat Assessment: AI-driven risk analysis and threat prioritization
