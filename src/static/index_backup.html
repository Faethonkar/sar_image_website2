We combine operational military experience, academic excellence, and cutting-edge research to deliver impactful solutions across multiple domains, including:

Innovation Initiatives

  • Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) imaging & low-RCS target detection
  • AI-driven Automatic Target Recognition (ATR)
  • Electronic Warfare and passive sensing
  • Unmanned systems and counter-drone technologies
  • Cyber threat intelligence and red teaming
  • Defense-oriented high-performance computing (HPC)

Our team is composed of seasoned professionals from the Hellenic Armed Forces, postdoctoral researchers, and engineers trained in institutions like NASA JPL, NTUA, Cranfield University, and USC.

We support military, governmental, and industrial partners by bridging research and real-world application—delivering strategic insight, robust consulting, and deployable technologies that align with evolving security needs.

At DBF, we don’t just study the fight—we enable those who must win it.
