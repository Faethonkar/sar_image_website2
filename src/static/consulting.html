About Our Firm

DBF Science Technology delivers specialized defense consulting services to government agencies, armed forces, and the defense industry. With expertise in advanced technologies and operational analysis, we transform complex security challenges into actionable solutions for operational superiority.

Core Competencies

Defense Strategy & Operational Planning

  • Threat Analysis & Risk Assessment: Comprehensive evaluation of geopolitical and operational threats
  • Force Structure Optimization: Strategic alignment of personnel, equipment, and capabilities
  • Operational Doctrine: Development and modernization of tactical and operational frameworks
  • Wargaming & Simulation: High-fidelity scenario analysis and operational exercises
  • Modernization Programs: Multi-year strategic planning for capability transformation

Technology Integration & Innovation

  • Defense Technology Assessment: Evaluation and selection of ISR, EW, C4I, and AI systems
  • Operational Integration: Incorporation of new technologies into existing structures
  • Cost-Benefit Analysis: ROI and operational value assessment for defense investments
  • AI & Autonomous Systems: Strategy for machine learning and robotic systems
  • Cybersecurity & INFOSEC: Protection of critical infrastructure and networks

Operational Intelligence & Analytics

  • GEOINT & SAR Analysis: Consulting for satellite imagery and processing systems
  • Intelligence Architecture: Design of collection, analysis, and dissemination systems
  • Big Data Exploitation: Transforming data into operational intelligence
  • Predictive Analytics: Forecasting models and early warning systems

Special Operations & Maritime Defense

  • Maritime Surveillance & MDA: Systems for monitoring and protecting maritime zones
  • Counter-Drone & C-UAS: Strategies for detecting and countering unmanned systems
  • Counter-IED & EOD: Techniques for addressing improvised explosive devices
  • Specialized ISR Systems: Advanced reconnaissance and surveillance capabilities
